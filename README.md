Game Name: A Treasure in the Hut

Genre: 3D Horror, Puzzle

Elevator Pitch: In a dark and eerie setting, the player embarks on a thrilling quest to find treasure, racing against time. They must locate a crucial key hidden in the main house, reach the terrifying shed in the garden, and retrieve the treasure inside. Racing against a tight deadline, failure to find it in time will bring a surprise.

Core Loop: Explore Environment , Find Hidden Key, Unlock Cabin, Retrieve Treasure, Win

Controls:

 WASD: movement

Mouse: look around

Shift:  toggle sprint

E : Interact/ open door/ take key/ open treasure

Win-loss conditions: The player wins if they reach the treasure in time, otherwise they lose and a jumpscare appears.

Art style : Realistic, modern.
